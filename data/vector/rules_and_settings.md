# Fantasy Football Draft Basics

All fantasy seasons begin with the Draft, when team managers take turns selecting players for their teams. At NFL.com, you may practice for this event using Mock Drafts, or even join a league using the Live Draft Lobby.

The draft client features the draft order, a timer, stats, analysis, your queue, a history of who has been selected, team rosters, and a chat room for the league. Search for players by using the search bar or by scrolling the player list, filter them by position, or insert and rank players in a queue that will update as players are taken.

# Draft Formats and Types

**Standard Draft**: Team managers take turns selecting players in a linear or snake draft order.

**Salary Cap Draft**: Team managers nominate players using an allotted budget. This does not involve using actual money.

**Keeper Draft**: Keepers are players that teams choose to draft again for following years in custom leagues where this option is enabled. During the Draft, NFL Fantasy distributes keepers first, but commissioners can use the Custom Draft Board to distribute them in later rounds.

**Live Online Draft**: Team Managers all draft at the same predetermined time, online, using NFL.com's draft client.

**Offline Draft**: Team managers in a Custom League hold a draft without use of the Draft Client or Autopick, and the Commissioner manually enters the selections to begin the season.

# Draft Order Types

**Linear Draft**: For every round in the draft, team managers draft or nominate in the same order.

**Snake Draft**: Team managers draft their teams in an order that alternates forwards and backwards from round to round. The team manager with the last pick of the first round gets the first pick of the second, and so on.

**Autopick Draft**: Team managers unable to attend part or all of the draft have their teams automatically drafted by the site during their absence according to personal or site pre-draft rankings. In Autopick Leagues, the entire league is auto-drafted.

The order of the draft is either determined at random by NFL.com 30 minutes prior to the draft or, in Custom Leagues, by the Commissioner.

# Live Draft Lobby

From the Live Draft Lobby, select the Draft Now button to be placed into the next available draft, which you can enter once enough other team managers have signed up. After the draft, the league begins. All Live Draft Lobby leagues are NFL-Managed and will have default settings and rules.

Thirty minutes before a draft, "Draft Now" appears on the league homepage and in the Fantasy Draft Center. Custom Leagues note: if there are not managers for every team slot within an hour of the draft, the draft will automatically be postponed.

# League Types

**NFL-Managed Leagues** are leagues created and auto-run by NFL.com with standard/default settings for scoring, rosters, transactions, seeding, and schedule. Fantasy players like these leagues for their simplicity, because the majority of fantasy football strategy content is geared towards leagues with default settings, and because no team manager has any power over the other.

**Custom Leagues** are leagues created and run by an NFL.com user with customizable scoring, roster, transaction, seeding, division, and schedule settings. Some users find these leagues more exciting, as they have more freedom to enjoy scoring and settings other than those which are found in NFL-Managed Leagues.

**Autopick Leagues** are drafted automatically according to site or personal rankings.

**Winners Leagues** are accessible only to those users that have previously won a Fantasy League at NFL.com.

# League Settings Options

**Teams**: Default 10, Custom 4-20 (even numbers only)
**Divisions**: Default None, Custom None or 2-4
**Roster Max (season)**: Default No Maximum, Custom No Maximum/None Allowed/1-100
**Roster Max (week)**: Default No Maximum, Custom No Maximum/None Allowed/1-25
**Max Trades per Season**: Default No Maximum, Custom No Maximum/None Allowed/1-50
**Trade Deadline**: Default No Deadline, Custom No Deadline or Several Options
**Trade Review Type**: Default League Votes, Custom None/Commissioner Veto/League Votes
**Waiver Period**: Default 1 Day, Custom No Waivers or 1-4 Days
**Waiver Type**: Default Resets to Inverse Standings Order (weekly), Custom options include Move to Last After Claim, Never Reset, or Waiver Budget
**Keeper Settings**: Default None, Custom None or 1-40 Players

# Default Roster Settings

**Default Roster**: 9 starters (QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, W/R Flex, TE, K, DEF) and 6 bench

**Custom Roster Options**: Up to 40 players with positional limits including QB, RB, WR, TE, WR/RB Flex, WR/TE Flex, QB/WR/RB/TE Flex, RB/WR/TE Flex, K, DEF, DL, LB, DB, Flex IDP, Bench, Reserved Slot

**Position Designations**: QB, RB, WR, TE, FLEX (W/R), FLEX (W/T), FLEX (R/W/T), FLEX (Q/R/W/T), K, DEF, DL, LB, DB, FLEX IDP (DP)

**The FLEX Position**: Most leagues feature a "flex" slot in the starting lineup. This slot holds players of varying positions, allowing managers to start an additional position player on the roster. The most common and default type of FLEX designation holds either Wide Receivers or Running Backs.

# Default Scoring System

**Passing**: 1 point per 25 yards, 4 points per TD, -2 points per interception
**Rushing**: 1 point per 10 yards, 6 points per TD
**Receiving**: 1 point per reception, 1 point per 10 yards, 6 points per TD
**Other Offense**: 6 points for return TDs, 2 points for 2-point conversions, -2 points for fumbles lost
**Kicking**: 1 point for PAT, 3 points for FG (0-49 yards), 5 points for FG (50+ yards)
**Defense**: 1 point per sack, 2 points per interception/fumble recovery/safety, 6 points per TD, points allowed scale from 10 points (shutout) to -4 points (35+ allowed)

**Scoring Options**: Fractional points enabled by default, negative points enabled by default

# Custom Scoring Options

Additional scoring options available in custom leagues include:
**Offense**: Passing attempts/completions, incomplete passes, sacked, yardage bonuses (300-399, 400+ passing; 100-199, 200+ rushing/receiving), TD distance bonuses (40+, 50+ yards), return yards
**Kicking**: Missed field goal penalties by distance
**Defense**: Fumbles forced, blocked kicks, return yards, total yards allowed bonuses/penalties
**Individual Defensive Players**: Tackles, assisted tackles, sacks, tackles for loss, forced fumbles, pass defended, QB hits, return yards and TDs

**Bonus Rules**: TD yardage bonuses are cumulative (40+ and 50+ both apply to 55-yard TD). Total yardage bonuses are not cumulative (200+ bonus only, not 100-199 bonus, for 250-yard game).

# Scoring Corrections

Occasionally, the official statistics from a given game are reviewed by the NFL and changed to more accurately reflect what happened on the field of play. Under these circumstances, the scoring changes affects the point totals of fantasy players involved.

In the event of a scoring change, the results of a fantasy matchup, and thus standings and seeding, may change. The site will not manually change the outcome to reflect a previous, inaccurate result. You can see the latest scoring corrections by clicking on the Players tab and "Stat Corrections."

# Adding and Dropping Players

**Process**: Select the plus or minus icon next to the player from the player list, player card, or MY TEAM page. Available players are either free agents (added immediately) or "on the waiver wire" (added after the waiver period).

**Player Lock**: In most leagues players lock in position at game time or on waivers when they are dropped. Added players are placed on the bench.

**Free Agent vs Waivers**: Free agents are added immediately. All other players are locked "on waivers" and may be added after the "waiver period."

# Trading Players

**Process**: Select the trade icon (opposing arrows) next to a desired player from various locations including Team Pages, player lists, or player cards. Include comments and submit in the Review Trade Proposal page.

**Default Trading Rules**: No maximum trades per season, 2-day league voting period beginning overnight after trade acceptance, league majority (including traders) required to overturn, starting players locked at game time may delay trade processing until after the weekend.

**Trade Management**: View pending trades on MY TEAM page, cancel trades before acceptance using "Cancel Trade" button.

# Setting Your Lineup

**Process**: Setting your roster takes place in the MY TEAM tab (desktop) or TEAM tab (app). Select the gray box (desktop) or blue square (app) to move players between starting lineup and bench. Use up/down arrows or drag-and-drop to change positions.

**Lineup Requirements**: You can only start as many players as your league settings allow and only in the positions in which each player is designated.

**Saving Changes**: Select SUBMIT button on desktop to save moves; app saves automatically. Team managers may make changes until players lock at game time.

# Lineup Lock Times

Roster spots lock (players become unable to be moved, added, dropped, or traded) in one of three ways:

1. **Individual Game Time** (default): Each player/spot locks at about the individual player's listed kickoff time
2. **First Game of Week** (custom option): All players/spots lock at about the first game's listed kickoff time  
3. **No Lock** (custom option): Players/spots do not lock for games

Any Free Agent will switch to Waivers at the lock times above. If No Waivers is chosen, Free Agent Lock Type must be determined (Yes at game time, or None).

# Injury Designations

Players who are injured will have an Injury designation near their name. Final injury designations are made on game days:

**Inactive (IA)**: Players are officially inactive for the current game and will not play
**Out (O)**: Not scheduled to play  
**Doubtful (D)**: Players have approximately a 25% chance of playing
**Questionable (Q)**: Players have approximately a 50% chance of playing

The Injury Report in the Player tab shows official injury reports sortable by team, roster status, and week.

# Reserve Slot in Custom Leagues

The RESERVE slot is a Custom League roster position option that allows an injured or suspended player to be kept on a team but not count as a bench spot or against position limits.

**Eligible Players**: Players officially labeled by their NFL team as IR (Injured Reserve), NON (Non Football-Related Injured Reserve), SUS (Suspended), PUP (Physically Unable to Perform), or EXE (Exempt).

**Reactivation**: For a reserved player to be moved back to the roster, the roster must have space and be within league-specified limits. A reactivated player remaining in the Reserve Slot will prevent transactions but will not prevent earning fantasy points.

# Tie Handling

**NFL-Managed Leagues**: Allow ties to be counted in team records if two opponents end a matchup with the same score.

**Custom League Options**: Select "No" to prevent ties, then choose a single-game tie-breaker: Bench Points, QB Points, RB Points, WR Points, TE Points, Defense Points, or K Points.

# Season Standings and Tiebreakers

Default tiebreakers for season standings in case of an overall W-L-T record tie:
1. **Points For** (total points scored)
2. **Head-to-Head Record** (win-loss-tie percentage in matchups against all other tied teams combined)

**Custom League Options**: Points For (default), Head-to-Head, Points Against, or Division Record (only available for leagues with divisions).

# League Power Rankings

Rankings shown in the LEAGUE homepage:
**Overall**: Rankings based on win/loss record
**Breakdown Rank**: Rankings based on records of hypothetical games against every team each week  
**Coach Rank**: Rankings based on difference between starting lineups and ideal lineups
**True Rank**: Rankings based on combination of all three previous rankings plus total points scored

# Playoff Seeding and Brackets

**Default Playoffs**: Top 4 teams in single-elimination tournament starting Week 15. Seeding: #1 vs #4, #2 vs #3. If playoff ties occur in leagues that allow ties, higher seed wins.

**Custom League Championship Options**:
- Week 15 and 16 (4 teams)
- Week 14, 15 and 16 (6 teams)  
- Week 15, 16 and 17 (6 teams)
- Week 14, 15, 16 (8 teams)
- Week 15, 16 and 17 (8 teams)
- Week 13, 14, 15 and 16 (10 teams)
- Week 14, 15, 16 and 17 (10 teams)
- Week 13, 14, 15 and 16 (12 teams)
- Week 14, 15, 16 and 17 (12 teams)
- No Playoffs
